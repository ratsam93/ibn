# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js / React (user's choice). This is the landing site; an attendee app and a backend will follow as separate surfaces in the same product family.

## Users

- **Delegates:** Indian-origin and India-linked CEOs, founders, investors and C-suite executives, chiefly UK/India based, deciding whether to apply for one of 100 invitation places.
- **Sponsors and partners:** corporates and institutions who may support the summit through the Treasurer's partner-relations function.

## Product Purpose

The Indian Business Network (IBN) runs the Global Indian Business Excellence Awards 2026: a 5-day, invitation-only leadership programme, 5-9 November 2026, opening at the House of Commons and continuing through a Black Tie Gala and forums at Oxford, Cambridge and Imperial College London. The landing site converts qualified visitors into applications and sponsor enquiries, and later fronts the delegate app and backend.

## Positioning

Capped at 100 distinguished guests, hosted inside the House of Commons, and joined to Oxford, Cambridge and Imperial for Indo-British business corridors. Attendance at the Awards ceremony carries no fee; delegate package fees relate only to the leadership programme, forums, hospitality and logistics.

## Operating Context

Application via Tally form (https://tally.so/r/NpzrWj), rolling and closing at capacity. Parliamentary security vetting needs full passport-matching legal names at least 30 days before the event. IBN issues UK Visa Support Letters. Secretariat contact: events@ibnonline.co.uk.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Invitation-only. Never imply tickets are sold or that the ceremony has a monetary value.
- Eight award categories; five programme days; delegate inclusions as listed on the source site.
- Sponsorship terms, prices and tiers are undecided and must not be invented; the sponsor path routes to the secretariat by email.
- Future surfaces: attendee app (encrypted digital certificates are already promised onsite) and a backend. The landing must be structured as the first route of a multi-surface product.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Indian Business Network (IBN); event: Global Indian Business Excellence Awards 2026.
- Identity: the IBN lotus logo (red, indigo, green, saffron petals) and the navy-and-gold palette of the existing site are kept and elevated (user decision).
- Tagline: Connect, Collaborate, Inspire. Pillars: Honour, Connect, Inspire.

## Evidence on Hand

Source site https://sentientiaproject.github.io/ibn-london-2026/ with full copy, programme, FAQ and secretariat contacts; logo, Big Ben imagery, four institutional crests (low resolution), four leadership portraits, a 15s 16:9 advert and 9:16 reel (MP4), a poster PDF. Assets downloaded to `public/img/`. No testimonials, past-winner lists, sponsor logos, or pricing exist and none may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Exclusivity is stated as fact (100 places, parliamentary vetting), never as hype.
2. Every claim on the page traces to IBN's own copy.
3. Two audiences, two clear paths, one shared credibility story.
4. The page is the front door of a product family, so structure and tokens must extend to an app and backend.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast on the dark ground; reduced-motion honoured; the audience includes senior executives on phones and desktops.
